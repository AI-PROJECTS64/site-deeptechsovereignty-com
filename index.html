DeeptechSovereignty.com

Descriptive .com domain for the emerging concept of “deep tech sovereignty”: the ability of States, investors and industrial ecosystems to design, finance, manufacture and secure critical deep technologies — semiconductors, advanced compute, AI, quantum, biotech, new space, defence tech — without excessive structural dependence on external suppliers.
DeeptechSovereignty.com is conceived as a neutral, institution-friendly banner for alliances, observatories, indices and public–private programmes dedicated to deep tech sovereignty, technological sovereignty and strategic autonomy.
It can host board-level narratives and frameworks reconciling national security, competitiveness, industrial policy and capital allocation across deep tech domains, while remaining above any single vendor, fund or national brand.
Intended for governments, sovereign and deep tech funds, large industrial and financial groups, development banks, alliances and think-tanks that need a clear and durable label for deep tech sovereignty strategies over the 2025–2035 horizon.
Serious institutional enquiries only (States, public agencies, deep tech / sovereignty investors, multilateral institutions, large corporates, alliances, foundations).

Il permet de porter, au-dessus des marques et des offres commerciales, une narration lisible par les Conseils, ministères, fonds, autorités de défense, industriels et grandes institutions financières sur l’horizon 2025–2035.
Destiné en priorité aux États, agences publiques, fonds deeptech / souveraineté, banques de développement, grands groupes industriels & financiers, alliances et think tanks qui recherchent une bannière internationale crédible et institutionnelle.
